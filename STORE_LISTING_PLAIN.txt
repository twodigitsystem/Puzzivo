Puzzivo — Think. Play. Repeat.

Welcome to Puzzivo, the ultimate all-in-one brain training puzzle collection! Puzzivo brings 11 popular puzzle and logic mini-game genres into one clean, beautifully designed experience.

Whether you love numbers, card memory challenges, rapid math calculations, hidden word searches, or classic logic grids, Puzzivo has something for every puzzle enthusiast — 100% playable offline with no internet required!

11 FUN MINI-GAMES IN ONE APP

1. Number Merge (4x4 Slide & Combine)
Slide matching number tiles across a 4x4 grid. Combine 2s, 4s, 8s, 16s, and beyond to chase your highest score. Use the single-step Undo feature to rethink your strategy!

2. Memory Match (Card Pair Challenge)
Test and sharpen your short-term memory! Flip hidden cards on a 4x4 grid to find all 8 matching pairs with the fewest moves possible.

3. Quick Math (60-Second Timed Arithmetic)
Put your calculation speed to the test! Solve addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division problems before the 60-second timer hits zero.

4. Word Search (Hidden Letter Grids)
Search for hidden words inside themed letter grids! Connect letters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally across 20 unique offline puzzle packs.

5. Sudoku (Classic 9x9 Logic Grids)
Fill 9x9 grids with numbers 1 to 9 without repeating any digit in rows, columns, or 3x3 boxes. Test your logical reasoning!

6. Minesweeper (Classic Minefield Grid)
Uncover safe tiles without detonating hidden mines! Use numerical hints to deduce safe paths across the minefield.

7. Nonogram (Picture Cross Logic)
Use row and column number clues to fill in grid cells and reveal hidden pixel art shapes in this popular Japanese logic puzzle.

8. Hex Merge (Hexagonal Board Combine)
A fresh hexagonal twist on number merging! Connect matching numbers across a 6-sided grid to unlock higher values and maximize your score.

9. Lights Out (Grid Light Toggle)
Tap grid cells to toggle surrounding lights on and off. Can you turn off all the lights in the fewest moves?

10. Pattern Recall (Sequence Memory Test)
Watch the light sequence flash across the grid and repeat the pattern in exact order. How long a sequence can you memorize?

11. Word Scramble (Anagram Puzzle)
Unscramble jumbled letters to discover hidden target words. Expand your vocabulary with endless word challenges!
