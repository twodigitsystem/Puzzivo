Privacy Policy for Puzzivo

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Welcome to Puzzivo ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing an enjoyable mini-game and brain puzzle experience. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application (Puzzivo) handles your information, what data third-party services may collect, and how you can exercise control over your privacy.

By installing and using Puzzivo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

Puzzivo is designed as an offline-first, standalone mobile application:

  • No User Accounts: We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal identification information such as your name, phone number, physical address, or email address to play our games.
  • No Custom Server Data Uploads: Your puzzle scores, best move counts, daily streaks, completed puzzle progress, and in-app preferences (sound and haptics) are stored in the app's local Android DataStore. They are never transmitted to or stored on external servers managed by us. Android device backup or device-transfer services may copy this local app data according to your device settings and Google's backup controls.

2. Information Collected by Third-Party Services (Google AdMob & UMP)

Puzzivo is a free, ad-supported application. We use Google Mobile Ads (Google AdMob) to display banner and rewarded video advertisements and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to handle user consent requirements.

While Puzzivo does not harvest or process personal data directly, the Google Mobile Ads SDK integrated within the app may automatically collect and process certain information from your device for ad delivery, personalized advertising, fraud prevention, and analytics.

Data processed by Google AdMob includes:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (GAID), Android ID, unique device identifiers.
  • IP Address: Used to estimate general geographic location (e.g., country or city level) for relevant ad serving.
  • App & Ad Usage Data: Ad views, clicks, ad interaction metrics, and performance data.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: Crash logs, load times, technical performance metrics, and SDK error diagnostics.

For detailed information on how Google collects, uses, and shares data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Mobile Ads Privacy & Data Safety Documentation

3. Advertising and Personalized Ads

Banner and Rewarded Ads:

  • Banner Ads: Non-intrusive banner ads are displayed in designated areas at the bottom of screens.
  • Rewarded Ads: Optional rewarded video ads allow you to earn in-game benefits (e.g., Undo Last Move in Number Merge, Reveal Pair in Memory Match, +15 Seconds in Quick Math, or Reveal Word in Word Search). Watching a rewarded ad is entirely voluntary.

Managing Your Ad Preferences and Advertising ID:

Depending on your region (such as the EEA, UK, or California), you will be presented with a consent choice dialog upon app launch managed by Google UMP. You can manage or revoke your ad personalization consent preferences at any time.

Additionally, you can opt out of interest-based advertising or reset your device’s Advertising ID via your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Privacy or Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

4. Permissions Requested

Puzzivo requests minimal Android system permissions strictly necessary for core functionality and ad serving:

  • INTERNET: Required by Google AdMob to load advertising content.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check network availability for ad loading without disrupting offline gameplay.
  • VIBRATE: Required to provide subtle tactile (haptic) feedback when interacting with game tiles and buttons (can be toggled off in App Settings).

5. Children's Privacy

Puzzivo is designed for a general audience of casual puzzle players. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Game Data: Since all game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device, you can completely erase all app data at any time by clearing the application data in Android Settings > Apps > Puzzivo > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Third-Party Data: Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's standard data retention policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
