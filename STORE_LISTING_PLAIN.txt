Puzzivo — Think. Play. Repeat.

Welcome to Puzzivo, a compact collection of fun, fast puzzle games for quick breaks and daily challenges. Puzzivo brings four popular puzzle genres into one clean, beautifully designed experience.

Whether you love numbers, card memory challenges, rapid math calculations, or hidden word searches, Puzzivo has something for every puzzle enthusiast — 100% playable offline with no internet required!

4 FUN GAMES IN ONE APP

1. Number Merge (4x4 Slide & Combine)
Slide matching number tiles across a 4x4 grid. When two identical numbers touch, they merge into double their value! Combine 2s, 4s, 8s, 16s, and beyond to chase your highest score. Reached a tricky spot? Use the optional single-step Undo feature to rethink your strategy!

2. Memory Match (Card Pair Challenge)
Test and sharpen your short-term memory! Flip hidden cards on a 4x4 grid to find all 8 matching icon pairs. Track your total moves and completion time. Can you beat your personal best with the fewest flips possible?

3. Quick Math (60-Second Timed Arithmetic)
Put your mental calculation speed to the test! Solve as many addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division problems as you can before the 60-second timer hits zero. Each correct answer earns points and builds your high-score streak. Need more time? Grab a quick +15 Seconds bonus!

4. Word Search (Hidden Letter Grids)
Relax and search for hidden words inside themed letter grids! Connect letters horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to complete target word lists. Features 20 unique offline puzzle packs covering Animals, Space, Fruits, Weather, Tech, and more!
