Privacy Policy & Data Safety for Puzzivo

Last updated: November 4, 2026 | Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Welcome to Puzzivo ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing an engaging, high-quality brain training mini-game experience. This Privacy Policy explains how Puzzivo processes your information, how we comply with Google Play Data Safety requirements, and how you can manage your data.

1. Overview & Architecture

Puzzivo is built with an offline-first local storage model supplemented by optional Google Cloud services for global leaderboards:

  • Local Gameplay Data: Your puzzle scores, levels, coins, gems, daily streaks, unlocked themes, and game settings are saved locally on your device using Android Room Database and DataStore. This data is private to your device and is never uploaded to private servers or sold to third parties.
  • Optional Google Sign-In & Leaderboards: If you choose to sign in with Google to participate in global leaderboards, Firebase Auth processes your public display name and profile photo URL to publish your scores on Cloud Firestore. Anonymous authentication is used to keep leaderboard reads isolated and secure.

3. Third-Party SDK Integrations

Puzzivo integrates trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google to support monetization, analytics, and cloud features:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays non-intrusive banner ads and voluntary rewarded video ads. AdMob collects device identifiers and coarse location to serve relevant ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Manages privacy choices and user consent in accordance with GDPR (EEA/UK) and CCPA/CPRA regulations.
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects anonymous diagnostics and performance telemetry to detect and fix crash issues across Android device models.
  • Firebase Remote Config: Dynamically adjusts game balance parameters (e.g. daily rewards, XP multipliers) remotely without uploading personal data.

Review Google's Privacy Disclosures:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information From Partner Apps
  • Google Mobile Ads Data Safety Documentation

4. Data Security & Encryption

  • Data in Transit: All network communication between Puzzivo and Google Firebase/AdMob services is encrypted using Industry-Standard HTTPS/TLS 1.3 protocol.
  • On-Device Isolation: Local game progress is saved in app-private storage protected by Android system sandboxing.

5. Advertising Preferences & Consent Controls

You have full control over advertising choices:

  • Consent Settings Menu: In EEA/UK regions, you can update or revoke your Ad Consent preferences anytime via Settings > Ad Privacy Choices inside Puzzivo.
  • Resetting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID on your Android device via Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID.

6. Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Google Play User Data policies, you can request full deletion of your data at any time:

  • Delete Local Data: Open Puzzivo, go to Settings > Reset High Scores & Progress, or clear app storage via Android Settings > Apps > Puzzivo > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Delete Leaderboard & Account Profile Data: If you signed in with Google and wish to delete your Cloud Firestore leaderboard entry and user profile, send an email request to privacy@puzzivo.app with your Google Account display name. Your cloud records will be permanently removed within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K Compliance)

Puzzivo is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). If we learn that personal data of a child has been collected without parental consent, we will take prompt steps to delete such data from our databases.
